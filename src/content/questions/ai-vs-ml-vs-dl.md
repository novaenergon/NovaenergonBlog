Artificial Intelligence (AI), Machine Learning (ML), and Deep Learning (DL) are related but not the same.  
Think of them as **nested circles**:

- **AI** → The broad concept of machines simulating human intelligence.  
- **ML** → A subset of AI where systems learn from data.  
- **DL** → A further subset of ML using deep neural networks for advanced tasks.
